# ARCANA Packages Phase Specification

ARCANA's next phase makes the six approved packages functional. The package catalog remains after the core reading, but each package becomes a real spread with a fixed position list and exact card count.

Packages:
- The Question: 3 cards — Question, Challenge, Guidance
- The Connection: 5 cards — You, The Other, The Dynamic, The Tension, The Guidance
- The Path: 5 cards — Current Ground, Strength, Obstacle, Opportunity, Guidance
- The Crossroads: 5 cards — Current Situation, Option A, Option B, What to Consider, Guidance
- The Year Ahead: 12 cards — January through December
- Deep Dive: 10 cards — Core, Challenge, Root, Past, Present, Near Influence, Self, Environment, Hope/Fear, Guidance

The user selects every card. No package may auto-draw cards. Duplicate cards are prohibited. Orientation is independently randomized. Interpretations use the actual selected card, orientation, position, and user question. Package synthesis is descriptive/reflection-oriented and must not become deterministic prediction.
